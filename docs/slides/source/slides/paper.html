Not like-for-like

31 vs 37 tags, rebuilt prompts, newer model

Macro F1 in percent. The paper's only tag baseline is GPT-3.5 asked zero-shot, on Python: 29.5 from the code, 33.7 with the description, averaged over its 31 tags on its validation split. It reports no per-tag score, so no focus-8 number. We rebuilt both prompts, since the paper never shows them, and sent them to gpt-6-luna: 47 to 52 over all 37 tags, and 63 to 74 on the 8 focus tags. On test, the all-tag column counts the one tag with no test problem, schedules, as zero, for every row. Same direction as the paper: the problem statement helps, by 3.4 points on our test against 4.3 in the paper. But this is context, not a leaderboard: other tags, other problems, one solution per problem instead of many, rebuilt prompts and a model three years newer, and our problems were public before our model's cutoff, as the paper's mostly were before ChatGPT's. Even an untuned logistic regression on the statement matches the paper's GPT-3.5 out of fold. Our best focus-tag method reaches 75.1 on test.
