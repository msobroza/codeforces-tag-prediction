The split balances the 9 frequent pairs

Each row is a tag; each cell is the share of that tag's problems that also carry the column tag, so the diagonal is 100%. Read it by rows. Math is the hub: three quarters of number theory problems, half of probabilities, a third of geometry and more than a quarter of games are also tagged math. The relation is not symmetric: math is so frequent that only 19% of math problems are number theory. Graphs and trees form the second pair: 38% of trees problems are also graphs, and 23% of graphs problems are trees. Strings stands apart, sharing under 10% of its problems with any other focus tag. Because one problem often needs two tags, every tag gets its own model and threshold, and the split balances the nine pairs shared by 20 or more problems.
