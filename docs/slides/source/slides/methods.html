From the simplest to the most recent. The label prior is the floor. TF-IDF is the tuned classical baseline, trained from scratch: 203 Optuna trials over statement and code features, ending on linear SVMs. ModernBERT is fine-tuned end to end, either with LoRA on the large model or fully on the base model, and bagged over the five folds. F2LLM keeps a 0.6-billion-parameter embedding model frozen and learns only a linear head, alone or fused with TF-IDF. The zero-shot methods send the xCodeEval paper's Code2Tag and DesCode2Tag prompts to OpenAI's gpt-6-luna, with no reasoning and temperature 0.32, close to the paper's 0.325, in free-text and JSON output. TypeSafe Jev is asked one yes/no question per tag, with a written definition of the tag, and its probabilities go through the same cross-fitted thresholds as every score method.
